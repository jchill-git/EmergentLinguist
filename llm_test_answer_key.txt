1. The war was over, but the people of the town did not play music, and they did not dance. The army had gone to the war, and many of the young men had not returned. The square had no one in it, and every window was dark.

2. One hour before the sun was low in the sky, a traveler came into the town. He was a very old man with a black case in his hand. No one saw him come. He went to the square, set the case on the road, and opened it. In it was a saxophone. He held it, and then he began to play.

3. The sound was slow and low. At first no one came, and the old man played to the sky. Then a child came near him. Then a lady opened her window. The people of the town came to the square, one by one, and they gathered around the traveler and heard the song.

4. When the first song ended, no one spoke. Then an old trumpeter, who had played in the town orchestra many years before, came out of his home with his instrument in his hands. He saw the traveler, and the traveler saw him. Together they began a second song, and this one was strong and bold.

5. A young man began to dance, and then a lady, and then a child. Then the square was full of people. The sun was gone, the moon came up, and they played on.

6. When the music ended, the traveler closed his case and left the town. No one knew his name. But from that hour, there was music in the square.
